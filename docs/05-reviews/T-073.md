## Points checked (no issue)
- **Conservative behaviour:** `needsReview` is always true; confidence 700 base, probed outputs 504-700; direction, last4 and merchant stay null when unsure (`transferred to` gives null + `directionUnknown`; a bare 4-digit number is never last4; lowercase Latin spans rejected; no currency fallback for `ريال`, `دينار`, `pound`, `Rs.`). Balance numbers, dates and times (`11:14`, `10/11`, ISO dates), `1.250,00`, `1,25.00` and absurdly large amounts abstain. Decline words abstain with `genericDeclined`.
- **ISO-code stop list:** sensible; `paid 50 all` does not become ALL. See nit 9.
- **Known institutions never fall back:** the generic call is inside `institution == null` only; drop stage runs before it (OTP from an unknown sender is dropped, tested).
- **Eligibility** matches platform-channels (alwaysCapture, share/shortcut/Apple Pay, senderHints and bodyHints together), tested with positive and negative cases.
- **AC2 rule** `genericDisposition` is pure and total (negative counts review).
- **T-033 follow-ups taken:** M-2 (probe over `drop`, `last4`, `globalDrop`, `globalLast4`, `bodyHints`, ladder to 4000; but see finding 1), M-5 (test renamed, mean printed, multi-penalty case 352 asserted), N-2 (enum length). Pipeline fuzz accepts `ParseGeneric`.
- **No text echo:** the result holds language, `Money`, direction, last4, cleaned merchant, receipt time and reason codes; the sentinel test and `toString` check cover it. Merchant display is the only free text and is the cleaned original span. Bodies are synthetic, no real SMS.
- **Safety:** scans are linear; 12 adversarial 4000-character bodies under 100 ms each; one small `RegExp` is compiled inside loops (`_candidates`, `_merchant`'s `endsClause`) which costs little (59-70 us mean) but could be hoisted.
- **Scope:** only parsing package, lint, docs and task file changed; the two public exports (`parseGeneric`, policy) are fine.
