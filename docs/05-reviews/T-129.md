## Declared deviations
- **New merchant also gets the text as an exact alias; merchantId given -> categoryCode ignored:** acceptable, and documented in backend-api §5.2. Without the alias a promoted merchant would never match in the app.
- **409 for non-live merchant/category, duplicate canonical, or non-alias text:** acceptable. Sound atomicity: one transaction under the draft lock, rollback includes the merchant created in the same call (tested). The race is handled correctly: the conditional UPDATE re-evaluates under READ COMMITTED after the lock wait (`TwoOperatorsDecidingAtOnceGiveOneWinner`).
- **Arabic-only text cannot be promoted yet:** acceptable for C2, not a major. It breaks no AC. The operator can still reject, and the queue is not blocked. See finding 1 for the recommendation.
- **CatalogAdminStore refactor:** minimal (`CreateMerchantInAsync`, `internal` on three helpers, DI double registration). The behaviour of `CreateMerchantAsync` is unchanged.
- **Tie-break by category code, reset of empty collecting aggregates to 0:** fine and tested.
