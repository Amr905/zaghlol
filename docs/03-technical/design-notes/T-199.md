# T-199 design note: net worth breakdown and balance per bank (C3, financial math, written by the implementer)

Scope: D-75, FR-WLT-01, BR-WLT-01, FR-CARD-02. Pure rules in `packages/zaghlol_domain/lib/wealth/` (balance rules, breakdown); SQL and wiring in `app/lib/features/wealth/data/`; screens in `wealth/presentation/`, Money tab and card detail. **No schema change** (no v6): the user-entered balance lives in a settings key (section 2).

## 1. Vocabulary and the one rule

A balance is always one (account, currency) pair, never summed across currencies (CLAUDE.md 2.4, FR-ACC-06). A multi-currency account has one pair per currency. Every line the breakdown shows has a **source**:

| Source | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `reported` | The `account_balances` row (written from a bank message), no counted transaction after it. |
| `userEntered` | The balance the user typed (section 2), no counted transaction after it. |
| `computed` | An anchor (reported or user-entered) plus the counted transactions dated after it. |
| `unknown` | No usable anchor. Left out of every total and listed as excluded with a "set balance" action. Never guessed, never zero. |
| (assets) | Live assets: manual value, certificate principal, gold in its own unit. Installments: remaining count x amount. |

The single rule: **balance now = newest known anchor + effect of the counted transactions dated strictly after the anchor.** Nothing else is ever added, and a pair with no anchor is not computed from zero (the history may be partial: a transaction list does not prove a starting point).

## 2. Anchor

Candidates for a pair:
- **Reported**: `account_balances(account, currency)` with `as_of` = UTC time of the message. For an asset account it is `balance_minor`; when that is NULL, `available_minor` is the anchor (Lead decision 2026-11-20: many Egyptian bank messages give only the available balance), with its own basis `reportedAvailable` and source text "Available balance from the bank message". For a credit or BNPL account it is the amount **owed**: `balance_minor` when present; otherwise `limit - available` when the account has a credit limit in the same currency and `available_minor` is present (floored at 0: a limit that lags behind an overpayment is not a negative debt); otherwise no anchor. `balance` wins over `limit - available` because it is the posted figure (available also holds pending authorisations); the card detail screen keeps showing used as `limit - available` (Lead decision 2026-11-20). Every account line carries its **basis** (`reportedBalance`, `reportedAvailable`, `limitMinusAvailable`, `userEntered`) and the source text says which figure was used.
- **User-entered ("opening") balance**: a figure the user types for a pair, with the instant it is true for (the Money tab and breakdown set it as "now"; the API takes any instant). Stored in the setting `balance.opening` as JSON `{"<accountId>":{"<CUR>":[minor, asOfMs]}}`: one pair, one entry, newest write wins, at most 200 entries and 16 KiB (the settings limit; a longer map is refused with a code). A settings key already travels in backup/restore and needs no migration. When an account is **merged**, its entries move to the target inside the same transaction (per currency the newer `asOf` wins, like the balance rows); an entry for a missing or merged account is ignored on read. The amount is in the pair's currency (no conversion); for a credit or BNPL account it is the amount owed.

The anchor is the candidate with the **newer instant**; on a tie the reported one wins (it is the bank's word). So typing a balance after the last message anchors on it; a later message re-anchors on the bank figure. Both candidates are kept: the older one is simply not used.

## 3. Which transactions move a balance

A transaction counts for its own (account, currency) pair, in `amount_minor` (the billed amount in the account currency; `original_amount_minor` of a foreign purchase is display only), when **all** hold: `deleted_at IS NULL`, `status = 'posted'`, and `occurred_at` is strictly after the anchor instant. Effect on the **cash side** (assets):

| Direction | Effect |
|---|---|
| `income`, `refund`, `transfer_in` | + amount |
| `expense`, `fee`, `transfer_out` | - amount |

For a credit/BNPL account the owed amount moves the other way (owed = anchor - cash-side effect): an expense or fee raises it; a refund, a payment received (`transfer_in`, the card side of a card payment) lowers it; a `transfer_out` from a card (cash advance) raises it.

Decisions that matter:
- **Own transfers are not excluded.** `is_internal` is an analytics flag (keeps the money out of spend/income). A paired transfer moves real money between two accounts: the out leg lowers A, the in leg raises B, so in one currency the two net worth effects cancel. A one-sided own-account leg (the other account is not tracked or its message has not come) moves only its own account. Filtering on `is_internal = 0` here would make a bank balance ignore money that left it.
- **Card payments** are two legs the same way: bank `transfer_out`, card `transfer_in` (kind `card_settlement`). If only the bank leg exists (no card-side message yet) the card's owed amount stays at its anchor until the card's next message re-anchors it, so net worth is understated by the payment meanwhile. Accepted residual, stated in the doc and the UI source line ("computed").
- **Refunds** raise the account they land on. `offset_links` (refund and reimbursement links) and splits only move *analytics*; they never touch a balance, so a refund is counted once (as the refund transaction itself), never again through its link.
- **Fees** lower the account. **FX** (`fx_conversion`, `cross_currency` pairs, foreign-currency legs of a multi-currency account): each leg affects the pair of its own currency; nothing is converted or matched across currencies. A transaction in a currency for which the account has no anchor gives that pair an `unknown` line. The pairs of an account are: its reported rows, its user-entered balances and every currency it has posted transactions in; an account with none of these is listed once in its default currency (so a USD-only Wise account does not get an empty EGP line).
- **pending / declined / reversed** never count (a pending hold is not yet a posted movement; the bank's own balance may already reflect it, hence no double count). A pending that later posts counts from then.
- **Hidden accounts count** (D-69); **merged accounts** are skipped (their rows were relinked to the target).
- **Out-of-order messages**: a transaction dated at or before the anchor is assumed already inside the anchor (a bank balance "after" the message includes everything up to it). A transaction dated exactly at the anchor instant is therefore not added again. Residual: a transaction that really happened after the balance but carries an earlier or equal timestamp (minute-level SMS times) is not added until the next message re-anchors.
- Overflow of any sum throws `NetWorthOverflow` (a code, no amounts); the one number never wraps.

## 4. Staleness

`computed` lines always say which anchor they started from and how many transactions were added. A `reported` or `userEntered` line with **no** later transaction and an anchor older than **30 days** is marked stale (muted "old" tag with the date); it still counts. Staleness never drops a line.

## 5. Net worth and exactly what is excluded

Per unit (currency code or gold symbol), `net = assets - liabilities`:
- **Assets**: asset-kind accounts (debit, account, wallet, cash, multi_currency) with a known balance (signed: an overdrawn account lowers assets, as today), live assets (certificate = principal, others manual value falling back to principal), gold natively in grams x 1000 / coins x 1000 (valued gold stays a separate figure, unchanged).
- **Liabilities**: credit and BNPL accounts with a known owed amount (a negative one is an overpayment), live assets flagged liability, and remaining active installment items (total - paid) x amount, **except** an item whose own credit/BNPL account already has a counted owed amount in that currency (not counted twice; this now uses the computed amount).
- **Excluded and listed**: every `unknown` pair; archived assets; merged accounts (not listed, they are not accounts any more); an asset with no value (as today). Excluded lines are listed in their currency's section; they are in no total.
- **Gold gating** (FR-WLT-01 "Free: accounts/wallets/cash only"): a free user's breakdown and Money tab figure cover accounts only, with credit and BNPL accounts as liabilities (they are accounts; this fixes the Money tab figure that ignored cards). Live assets, asset liabilities and installment items need Gold. `NetWorthRepository.compute` (snapshots, assets section) keeps including everything.
- Every line of the breakdown comes from the same list as the figure: `computeNetWorth` is fed the breakdown's included lines, so `sum(assets lines) - sum(liability lines) = unit net` by construction, and a unit test asserts it per currency.

Changes of numbers versus today, for the PO: (a) accounts without a fresh reported balance now count when an anchor exists; (b) a credit card with only an available figure and a limit now counts as a liability; (c) the Money tab figure now subtracts cards (BR-WLT-01) and, for Gold, includes assets.

## 6. Surfaces

- **Breakdown screen** (tap the Money tab figure): per currency: Accounts (bank, then its accounts; bank total = sum of its asset accounts in that currency), Assets, Liabilities, Excluded; each line amount + source + date; a bank/account line opens its transactions (the card filter with that bank's accounts); an excluded line has "set balance".
- **Money tab**: account rows show the effective balance and its source line; a bank header with the bank total appears when a bank has two or more asset accounts in the group. **Card detail** shows the effective balance, source and date.
- All amounts through `MoneyFormat.format(..., hidden:)`, a11y through `a11yAmounts`; strings in both ARB files; the used-share bar logic of T-197 is untouched.

## 7. Tests

Pure: anchor choice (reported vs user, tie), asset/liability signs for every direction, credit `limit - available`, no-anchor unknown, staleness, overflow. SQL (in-memory DB): reported then newer reported; refund; paired and one-sided transfers; card payment legs; fee; multi-currency account; pending/declined/reversed/deleted ignored; hidden counts, merged skipped; merge moves the user balance; breakdown sums equal `computeNetWorth` per currency; unknown listed as excluded. Widgets: breakdown ar/en, hidden amounts, light/dark goldens.

## Decisions (Lead, 2026-11-20)
1. Cards: posted `balance` (owed) else `limit - available`; card detail "used" unchanged; the line's source text names the basis.
2. Asset accounts with only an available figure anchor on it (fallback after `balance`), own source label.
