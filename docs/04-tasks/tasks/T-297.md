## Scope (artboard "4 · While working")
1. **Reading messages** (first SMS inbox scan, card re-scan): progress card with the `scanning` moment (loops while the scan runs), «بقرا رسايل البنك…» and a live count «لقيت {n} معاملة لحد دلوقتي».
2. **Ask Zaghlol waiting**: the answer placeholder shows the `thinking` moment (small, ~48 px) next to the typing dots while waiting > 1 s. Do not touch the M9 LLM helper logic files; presentation only.
3. **Statement import, backup, restore**: progress shows the `counting` moment with «بعدّ الفلوس… {done} من {total}» when a count is known, else an indeterminate line.
4. Stop the loop the moment work ends; errors switch to `oops` (≤ 1.4 s) with the existing error copy and `ZHaptics.play(ZHapticPattern.oops)`.
