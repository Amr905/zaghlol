## Scope (file `packages/zaghlol_parsing/lib/ocr/receipt_parser.dart`)
1. **Cross-checks** over the labelled amounts found: (a) net/subtotal + VAT/tax (+ service) = total; (b) a tender line (card/visa/cash/paid, non-zero) equal to a candidate total; (c) sum of item line totals ≈ net or total. A candidate confirmed by a check wins over an unconfirmed one, whatever its position; if the picked total contradicts every available check, return it through the fallback template (confidence 400) so the screen marks it as a guess.
2. A total label that is a **table header** (line also holds `qty`/`item`/`u p`/`price`/`الصنف`/`الكمية`/`السعر`) is never a label.
3. Never build an amount by joining digits across cells or lines (`1558`-style runs): an amount token must be one OCR token; integers without decimals lose to decimal amounts when both exist; an amount larger than 3× the sum of item totals is rejected when item totals are readable.
4. Dates `dd-MM-yyyy` with `hh:mm AM/PM` on the next token.
5. Fixtures in `packages/zaghlol_parsing/test/ocr/restaurant/` (synthetic: generic restaurant and item names, changed phone/hotline): row order, column blocks, label/value on alternate lines, amounts-before-labels, unit price and total merged (`200.88200.88`), `Amount Due` split across lines, missing decimals from OCR (`43600`), and one where only tender = total survives. Each states the expected amount or the expected guess.

## Design note (builder)
`_pickTotal` collects every labelled total candidate (not just the last), then reads cross-check values with the same label/value search: net sales/subtotal, VAT/tax, service, card and cash tenders, and item totals (largest strong amount per item row). A candidate confirmed by net+VAT(+service), any tender, or the item sum wins over unconfirmed ones; otherwise the last candidate is kept, and it is returned through the fallback template (400) when it contradicts every available check (net+VAT, a card tender, or items above it; items count as "neutral" when no net+VAT exists, so a plain tax-less receipt is not downgraded). Candidates above 3x the item sum (>=2 readable items) are dropped, and integer candidates lose to decimal ones. If every candidate is dropped, a lone card charge at least as large as every item is offered as a guess. Header rows (`Item Qty U P Total`, or a lone `Total` cell among header cells) are never labels. `200.88200.88` style merged cells are split into two tokens (never joined).

## Builder assumptions
- "Net Sales" is a net label, not a total label (it no longer competes as a total).
- Cash tenders confirm a total but never contradict it (cash may exceed it); only card tenders can contradict.
- AM/PM after a time is honoured (12 AM = 00:xx), which also fixes receipts printing `03:30 PM`.
- Item sum is "readable" with at least 2 item rows.
