## Implementation notes (T-120 builder)

- Code: `app/lib/features/voice/{application,data,presentation}`. Entry `showVoiceLogger` (Add sheet "Say" via `quickAddSelected`); `AddSheet.voiceLeft` now defaults to `voiceLeftProvider` (Free: left of 15; Gold hides the line).
- Recognition: `speech_to_text` behind `SpeechPort`, the only file importing the plugin is `data/speech_to_text_port.dart`. First attempt is `onDevice: true` (ar_EG / en_US). If it reports unavailable, a consent sheet (D-42) asks; only "Allow" starts an online attempt, once per session, never remembered. Deny goes to Type (S-13). Auto-stop 2 s silence / 30 s max.
- Nothing is saved before Save. One confirmed recording = one `voice_usage` increment, after the rows are stored (A-14). Free limit rechecked before listening and again at Save. Audio and transcripts exist only in controller state; the voice feature imports no `dart:io`/HTTP (checked by `test/privacy/voice_permission_test.dart`).
- Permissions: Android `RECORD_AUDIO` + `<queries>` for `android.speech.RecognitionService`; iOS `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription` (Info.plist English, `InfoPlist.xcstrings` ar + en).
- Items on an account of another currency than the spoken amount are stored on the first account in that currency; with none, Save is disabled (nothing converted).

### Device checks (T-011 spike not built; do on real devices)
1. Android 13+ (API 33) with the Arabic (Egypt) offline pack: `onDevice: true` recognises ar_EG and the consent sheet does not show.
2. Android 12 and lower, and a phone without the Arabic pack: `onDevice: true` must fail (error_language_unavailable / not supported) and show the consent sheet; confirm no audio leaves the phone before Allow (network capture).
3. iOS 17+ ar-EG: on-device (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition`) works offline in airplane mode; if the locale has no on-device model the consent sheet shows.
4. Mic permission: first use shows the OS prompt; deny -> explainer; "Open settings" lands on the app's settings (currently reuses the notification settings opener; on Android that opens the app notification page, not app details -> follow-up).
5. 2 s silence and 30 s max stop listening; the sound level moves the pulse ring (Android dB range about -2..10, iOS about -50..0).
6. Check the Play data-safety form: audio is processed by the OS recognizer, not collected by the app.
