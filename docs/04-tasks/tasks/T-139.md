## Description
Data kept only as long as allowed (NFR-PRIV-06).

## Technical details
- Daily purge per backend-schema §4:
  - inactive installs > 12 months (purchases keep `install_id = NULL`; seats go vacant and keep `device_key_hash`; `installs.seat_key_hash` goes with the row);
  - tombstoned installs/accounts after 30 days;
  - `store_notifications` 90 days; contributions; bank requests;
  - `audit_log` rows with `action IN ('support_lookup','support_seat_move','support_detach','support_unfreeze')` **exactly 1 year** after creation (account deletion neither shortens nor extends); other audit rows 3 years;
  - `replay_guard` after `expires_at`;
  - `seat_claimants` older than 7 days;
  - `idempotency_records` after `expires_at`;
  - `installs.registration_key_hash` / `registration_body_sha256` cleared after 24 h.
- `purchase_seats.recent_rebinds` is pruned inline by the seat service (T-184), not here.
- IP truncation; no body logging; token hashing in logs (with T-138).
