## Design / spec pointers
- `docs/03-technical/llm-categorise.md` §2 rule 5 (honorifics, ≥ 2 000 names ar + en transliterations, business words).
- `docs/01-requirements/llm-categorise.md` §2 and OQ-LCAT-05 (public-domain sources).

## Description
Compile three UTF-8 lists, one entry per line, already folded with the app's Arabic folding (alef/ya/ta-marbuta, no diacritics or tatweel; English lower case): ≥ 2 000 common Egyptian given names in Arabic and their common English transliterations; the honorifics of §2 rule 5; ≥ 60 business words ar + en (the §2 list plus common shop types). Record sources and licence in the README.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A test loads each file: counts (names ≥ 2 000, business words ≥ 60), no duplicates, no empty lines, every line already folded (folding it again is a no-op).
2. No business word appears in the names list, and no entry contains a digit.
3. README names each source and states it is public domain or compiled from general knowledge; no copyrighted list copied wholesale.
4. Assets declared in `pubspec.yaml`; `flutter analyze` green.

## Notes for the builder
- The folding function lives in `packages/zaghlol_parsing` (FR-PAR-04); reuse it in the test.
- Names only; no surnames-with-business meaning (e.g. «الشفاء») in the names list.
