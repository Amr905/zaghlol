## Root cause (builder)
The PO's screen plays `app/assets/rive/zaghlol.riv` (generated by `tools/mascot_riv` from `docs/02-design/brand/zaghlol-mascot-rig.svg`), not the still. The rig's `wing` group (the whole raised arm, which rotates in the proud/thumbs-up mood) also carries the shirt-cuff tab at the shoulder/neck joint: the dark silhouette blob plus a light (`#d2e8d0`) and a green (`#a1cc98`) fill and an ink outline piece (about rig x 216-250, y 440-490). In the still the neck feathers cover it; as part of the wing it is painted over the chest, so it is the light patch with a dark outline in the PO's circle. Reproduced by rendering proud at 0, 0.3, 0.6 and 1.0 s with `tools/mascot_riv/verify/render.mjs` (visible even at rest, largest while the arm is raised).

Fix: `tools/mascot_riv/rig.py` `trim_cuff_tab` (used by `generate.py`) drops the tab's fill pieces and clamps the silhouette at the sleeve edge (x 218); nothing redrawn. `zaghlol.riv` regenerated (390006 bytes); `verify/check.mjs` 59/59 pass; re-rendered frames show a clean joint. Known remaining: a tiny dark wedge of outline stays at the neck where the sleeve ends, as in the approved still.

Home: the artboard keeps its top quarter empty for props (bird measured at 26%-99% of the height). `ZaghlolAvatar.trimHeadroom` (Rive only) shrinks the box to 0.76 of the height and clips the empty top; Home's proud bubble uses avatar 92 (was 64) with it.
