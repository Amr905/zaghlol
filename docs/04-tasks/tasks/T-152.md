## Description
Automatic backups for Gold; one manual slot for Free.

## Technical details
WorkManager/BGProcessingTask schedules daily/weekly/monthly; Free overwrite single slot; Gold keep 5; upgrade keeps free backup as first version, downgrade keeps newest (FR-BAK-09); 30-day reminder (A-67); failure notification; cancel on delete-everything (EC-29).

## Design note
Written by the implementer (short). `application/schedule.dart` holds pure policy (`isScheduleDue` in the user's local calendar, `inRetryBackoff` 6 h, `isReminderDue` 30 d then weekly, `backupKeepFor` via T-119 `GoldGate`) and `BackupScheduleService`, which drives a `BackupDestination` port (Drive adapter here; iCloud adapter when T-151's Dart service exists). The service never deletes: `keep` (>= 1) goes to the destination's verify-then-prune. Free: manual only, keep 1; Gold: schedule + keep 5. Upgrade/downgrade (FR-BAK-09) fall out of keep 5 -> 1 at the next backup. Settings: `backup.schedule|destination|last_success|last_failure|reminder_at`. Ports without a platform binding yet: `BackupWorkScheduler` (WorkManager/BGProcessingTask), `BackupAlertSink` (l10n + NotificationService), iCloud destination, app version; inert defaults in `schedule_providers.dart`. `backupExistsProvider` is the seam for T-104 `setupBackupExistsProvider` (FU-144). `cancelAll()` is for T-166 (EC-29).

Missing l10n keys: reminder alert title/body (suggest `notificationsAlertBackupReminderTitle/Body`); the failure alert reuses `notificationsAlertBackupTitle/Body`.
