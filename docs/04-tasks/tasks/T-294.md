## Scope
1. **Trigger**: a salary transaction (any currency, D-85) that has not been celebrated yet — from SMS/notification/statement, or a **cash income marked as salary**. Celebrate once per salary transaction (store the last celebrated salary id/day; no repeats on reinstall restore of the same salary, no celebration for salaries older than 7 days found by the first inbox scan).
2. **Cash salary**: in the manual add flow for income (cash or any account), a «ده مرتب؟» switch (default off; on when the category is Salary). On saving with it on, the transaction is a salary (same flag/category the SMS path uses, so payday automation D-81 also sees it).
3. **When**: shown on the Home screen at the next app start (or right after the cash save returns to Home), never over another screen or mid-task; at most once per app start.
4. **Sheet (artboard B)**: bottom sheet over Home with the Rive bird in the `payday` moment, falling coins (simple Flutter animation, ≤ 1.6 s), «وصل المرتب!», the amount in its own currency (`+29,700.00 EGP` / `+1,000.00 USD`), «زغلول حسبها: تقدر تصرف حوالي {perDay} في اليوم لحد المرتب الجاي.» (only when an EGP pay cycle and a per-day figure exist; else hide the line), buttons «خطّط الشهر» (opens budget) and «تمام» (closes). Tap outside / swipe down closes. Coins and bird beats follow `ZHaptics.timelineMs(ZHapticPattern.payday)` and `ZHaptics.play(ZHapticPattern.payday)` fires when the sheet opens.
5. Remove the old payday bubble from the Home mascot slot (the sheet replaces it).
6. Reduce motion: still bird, no coins, one light haptic tick. ar/en, RTL, 1.3×, light/True Black dark, screen-reader label announcing the amount.

- Widget tests ar/en: sheet content, per-day line hidden when unknown, buttons, reduce motion.
