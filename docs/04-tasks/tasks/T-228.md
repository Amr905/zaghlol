## Scope (FU-258 row in `docs/implementation-status.md`, `docs/05-reviews/batch-38.md`, `docs/04-tasks/tasks/T-227.md` notes)
1. **#4 Paid bill keeps its reminder:** when a bill/subscription/instalment is marked paid (from the app or the notification action) or a matching payment is captured, cancel its pending scheduled reminder(s) for that due date. Re-schedule the next one per the existing schedule rules.
2. **#3 Weekly summary needs the app opened on Friday:** schedule the weekly summary as an OS alarm/notification for Friday at the configured time (Android exact or inexact alarm per the app's existing permission stance; iOS a scheduled local notification) computed from on-device data at the last opportunity the app had, and refreshed whenever the app runs. It must respect hide-amounts (no amounts when hiding, FU-267 rules), quiet hours and the user's notification settings. If the content can't be computed without the app running, show a generic, amount-free nudge ("Your week is ready") instead.
3. **#1 Notification action intents:** route actions through a non-exported receiver/trampoline or add a per-notification nonce that the app checks, so another app can't fire "mark paid" (security Low).
## Acceptance criteria
1. Tests: marking paid (both paths) cancels exactly that reminder and keeps others; Friday summary scheduled/refreshed with correct content in hide-amounts on/off; an action intent without the right nonce/route is ignored (Kotlin unit test).
2. No financial data leaves the phone; notification text follows existing redaction rules.
3. Android unit tests (run locally if Gradle works, else push the same commit to `ci/T-228` and report app-ci); iOS changes via `ci/T-228`.
4. `dart analyze`, `dart format`, notifications/bills tests green; FU-258 row updated.

## Design note (builder)
- **Contract change (flagged):** one new Pigeon method `NotificationsHostApi.cancel(int id)` (documented in `platform-channels.md` 4c). Needed because `cancelScheduled()` is all-or-nothing. No permission change.
- **#4 paid bill:** `NotificationService.cancelAlerts` computes the platform ids of the dedupe keys (`due|item|dueDate|threshold`, thresholds 0/1/3 = coming up, overdue day 1, overdue day 3) and calls `gateway.cancel`. `cancelPaidReminders` is called from `BillsActions.markPaid` (the app button and the notification action both go through it; only when the payment really counted) and from the `auto_mark_paid` housekeeping job (a captured payment). Other items and the next due date keep their ids. The "fired" claims stay so a cancelled reminder never comes back. The next reminder is scheduled by the normal run.
- **#3 Friday summary:** the week's figures cannot be known before Friday and native code computes nothing, so the app schedules a generic, amount-free nudge ("Your weekly summary is ready. Open Zaghlol to see it.", the existing `...WeeklyHidden` text) for the next Friday 10:00 local at every start/resume (`weeklyNudgeProvider`, inside `alertsResumeProvider`). Same inexact alarm / pending request as quiet-hour alerts (no exact-alarm permission). `NotificationService.scheduleAt` honours the per-type toggle (off: cancels) and quiet hours (moves to the end). The text is identical with hide-amounts on or off, so nothing needs re-deriving. When the app runs on that Friday, the real summary cancels the nudge first. Residual: Android loses the alarm at reboot until the app next runs (as for all scheduled alerts); the nudge is not a computed summary.
- **#1 action intents:** already closed by L-1 (non-exported `NotificationTapActivity` trampoline forwards route and cancel id with a per-process token that `MainActivity` requires; `NotificationHandOffTest`: forged intents without the token, wrong token, wrong action, Recents relaunch are ignored). Nothing new needed; verified, not re-done.
