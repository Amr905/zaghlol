## Description
With several banks, the PO couldn't tell how the net-worth figure was made. Today `net_worth_repository.dart` adds the latest reported balance per account (`account_balances`, usually from the bank SMS), subtracts credit/BNPL balances and adds assets; accounts with an unknown balance are silently left out, and the screen shows only the total.

## Technical details
- **Breakdown screen** (tap the net-worth figure): per currency, three groups — Accounts (each bank, then each account/card under it), Assets (gold, certificates, cash, stocks), Liabilities (credit cards, BNPL) — each line with its amount and **source**: "من رسالة البنك · ١٨ نوفمبر", "محسوب من الحركات", "أدخلته بإيدك", or "مش معروف — حط الرصيد" (excluded, with a button to set it).
- **Balance per bank from transactions:** when an account has no reported balance, or the reported one is older than the newest transaction, compute it as the last known balance (reported or user-set opening balance) + transactions since then. Show the bank total = sum of its accounts. Clearly mark computed vs reported; never mix currencies.
- Same per-bank balances on the Money tab bank headers and card detail.
- Respects hide-amounts (T-187) and the ≈ cross-currency view rules.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every line of the breakdown sums to the shown net worth per currency (unit tests), with unknown balances listed as excluded.
2. Computed balances are correct across reported-balance updates, refunds, transfers between own accounts and multi-currency accounts (unit tests).
3. Each line shows its source and date; tapping a bank opens its transactions.
4. ar/en, light/dark goldens; RTL verified; no hard-coded strings.
