## Scope
1. Find the tips Zaghlol shows in his box/bubble today (Home mascot bubble, `homeMascot*` / tip keys in the ARBs, any rotation logic) and how one is chosen.
2. Add **at least 30 new tips** in Egyptian colloquial Arabic (fun, light, never preachy, per `docs/02-design/06-voice-and-tone.md`) with natural English versions, in three groups:
   - **How to use the app (≥ 12):** scan a receipt with the camera or share a photo to Zaghlol; hold the Add button for voice; say an expense out loud; link a card to its account; set the payday / "شهر المرتب"; import a bank statement; review cards (Keep/Edit/Ignore); search by a person's name for InstaPay; hide amounts; set a budget per category; mark a salary; the "I exchanged money" entry if it exists on this branch (else skip).
   - **Smart features (≥ 10):** Ask Zaghlol questions like "صرفت كام على الأكل الشهر ده؟"; voice entry understands Egyptian; automatic SMS reading and categorising; card due reminders from bank messages; payday detection; duplicate detection; receipt reading. Describe what the feature does — don't call it "AI" unless the app's own copy already does, and never promise what the app doesn't do.
   - **Money habits with a joke (≥ 8):** short, kind, funny; no shaming, no financial advice claims, no stereotypes.
3. Truthful and safe: every tip must describe a feature that exists on this branch (check the screen exists); never say data is sent anywhere; the privacy claim is "بياناتك المالية مابتطلعش من موبايلك", never "no server". The budget period is "شهر", never "دورة".
4. Rotation: no tip repeats until the others in its group were shown (persist the last-shown index in settings; no personal data), usage tips are preferred in the first two weeks after install, and a tip never covers an alert-type mascot message (budget warnings win).
5. Tapping a how-to tip opens the feature it describes when a route exists (deep link); otherwise it is just text.
6. Copy deck (`docs/02-design/copy-deck.md`) updated; ARB keys grouped together; lengths fit the bubble at 1.3× text scale in ar and en (widget test over all tips for overflow).

## Acceptance criteria
≥ 30 new tips in ar + en; overflow test over every tip at 1.0× and 1.3×, RTL and LTR; rotation unit test; each how-to tip's target route exists (test); no "دورة", no "AI" claims beyond existing copy; `flutter analyze` clean.
