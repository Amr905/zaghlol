## Description
Recurring money is tracked and reminded.

## Technical details
Detection (≥ 2 charges, 25–35 days, ±10%); confirm/dismiss; annual ×12; installments with counts; gameya manual; card due from S-43 due day; auto-mark paid (±2%, ±5 days); reminders 3 days before.

## Design note
Written by the builder (no separate architect note existed).

- **Domain** (`packages/zaghlol_domain/lib/recurring/recurring_rules.dart`, pure): `detectSubscriptions` groups posted expenses by (merchant_norm, account, currency), walks back from the latest charge while gaps are 25..35 days and each amount is within 10% of the previous one (integer math); a chain of 2 or more yields one suggestion (amount = latest charge, annual = x12 via `Money.times`, next due = +1 month with the day clamped). A group with an existing subscription item in any status (so also dismissed) is skipped. `advanceDue` / `nextDueOnOrAfter` keep the anchor day and clamp to short months. `matchPayment` (FR-SUB-05): same merchant key (or, without one, same account), same currency, within 2% and within 5 days of the due date; closest date, then closest amount, then id. `progressAfterPayment` counts the payment and completes counted items. `reminderIsDue`: from due minus `reminder_days` at 10:00 local to the due date.
- **App** (`app/lib/features/recurring/`): `RecurringRepository.detectAndSuggest` writes a `suggested` item and a `subscription_suggestion` review item (payload ids only) in one transaction; `confirm` / `dismiss` are conditional on `status = 'suggested'` and resolve the review item. A dismissed item stays as a row so it is not suggested again. `addManual` covers installments (counts), gameya (manual only), bills; `syncCardDues` creates/moves one `card_due` item per credit account with a statement due day (S-43). `autoMarkPaid` claims the occurrence with `INSERT OR IGNORE` (one winner), then bumps `paid_count` and the next due date, all in one transaction. `sendDueReminders` feeds `NotificationType.upcomingDue` (dedupe key = item id + due date; text from the caller's l10n).
- **Decisions**: a card due with unknown amount (0) matches by account only, and a payment landing on the card (`transfer_in`) counts for card dues because a card payment is an own-account transfer. Due dates are Gregorian local `YYYY-MM-DD` (T-019 Q2). Mascot trigger on confirmation and the UI (T-102, T-105, T-113) are not part of this task.
