## Scope (plan §4.3; file `packages/zaghlol_parsing/lib/generic/generic_parser.dart` and keywords)
1. Last-4 after `رقم` / `رقم الحساب` / `xxx1888` / `419*5015`; reject when followed by `مرجعي`/`ref`/`reference`, or a run longer than 8 digits unless masked.
2. Transfers (InstaPay or transfer events, in and out): the other party goes to a **counterparty** field (name as written, masks `م***` kept, no word limit up to the next end anchor `رقم مرجعي`, `ref`, `يوم`, `بتاريخ`, `on`), not to merchant. `ParseGeneric` gains `counterparty`; ingest stores it as the transaction's counterparty like templated InstaPay.
3. Balance and available limit after balance anchors (`المتاح`, `الرصيد المتوفر`, `الرصيد المتاح`, `available balance is`, `avail bal`, `available limit is`), glued currency (`4667.24EGP`) handled; no currency → inherit the amount's. Never used as the amount.
4. Expense cues `trx using card`, `has been used for`, `a trx`; check no income wording regresses.
5. Merchant start anchors (`في`, `at`, `من`) must not take a date, time, function word or link: `في حالة`, `من خلال`, `at 01:21`. Stop at a sentence period followed by space, not inner dots (`S.A.E`, `amazon.eg`).
6. A text that is only a T&C/fee footer ("a late fee of EGP 150.00 applies if…", no completed-transaction cue) is not a transaction; remove the `pendingUntil: 'T-243'` marker on that negative in `packages/zaghlol_parsing/test/po_suite/po_suite_test.dart`.
7. Cases 2 and 4 now give the right counterparty from `BanqueMisr`.
## Acceptance criteria
