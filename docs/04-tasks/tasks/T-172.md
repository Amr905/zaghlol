## Description
Critical journeys automated on devices, including the one-phone Gold store-sandbox plan.

## Technical details
- Android: adb SMS with app killed → Home < 10 s; notification capture; scan 10k < 60 s. iOS: intent → visible on open. Backup Android → iOS restore.
- **Store sandbox (testing-strategy §1), scripted where possible, manual otherwise, with a recorded result per case:** purchase; pending (cash); renew; grace; refund; revoke; restore on a new phone; cross-platform with sign-in; plan change from another phone inside and outside the cooldown (D-59/D-61(a)); sign-out pause (D-61(c)); iOS delete-and-reinstall and "Delete everything" restore without a move (D-55); third reinstall restore inside 30 days on the holding iPhone (over-cap rebind, never refused, window restarts; D-63/D-65(a)); third reinstall **sign-in** there for Play-bought Gold (refused with `restoreSuggested`, support path shown, no Restore; R5-3(b)); a second iPhone behind a user-CA proxy fed the holder's signed entitlement body (no Gold; R5-1); plan change from a phone not signed in to the owning account (`accountRequired`; R4-4); Android "Delete everything" warning; clock set back offline (D-60, EC-70); quick offline reboot without a false tamper (R4-3).
- Android Apple sign-in with Samsung Internet and Firefox as default browser (backend-api §4.6).
