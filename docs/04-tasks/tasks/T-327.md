## Description
`investBand(S, goldPrice?, thresholds)` → `nothingSaved | small | stocks | gold | goldUnknown`; `illustrate(S, prices, instruments, horizonMonths)` → typed rows: gold grams (centigrams, 2 decimals, karat, price date, stale), USD ≈ (rate date), certificate payout (EGP only), `noPrice(currency)` lines. Instruments are a closed enum; there is no index row (no licensed feed, OQ-ASK-02).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Saved EGP 4 500.00, 21K at 4 000.00/g → **≈ 1.12 g**; USD/EGP 50.00 → **≈ USD 90.00**; `cd_example_rate_bp` 2000 × 12 months → **EGP 900.00**.
2. Bands with G = EGP 1 000.00: 100.00 → `small`; 100.01 → `stocks` (no gold row); 999.99 → `stocks`; 1 000.00 → `gold` (0.25 g); S ≤ 0 → `nothingSaved`; `invest_min_egp` 200.00 → 150.00 is `small`; no gold price ever → 500.00 gives neither stocks nor gold, USD/certificate kept.
3. Non-EGP S follows §3.4 per currency, never converted for band comparison; a currency with no price gives `noPrice`.
4. Price older than 24 h → row flagged `stale`; still used.
5. **Negative proof 1:** static test: no `double` in `lib/ask/`; the band comparison is exact in minor units (a test at the boundary 999.99 vs 1 000.00 with prices that would mis-round in floating point).
6. **Negative proof 2:** the output types cannot carry a free-text instrument name (closed enum only; test enumerates the values: gold, usd, certificate).

## Notes for the builder
- Grams: integer centigrams, half-even once. Certificate: `S × bp × months ÷ (10 000 × 12)`, half-even once.

## Design note (builder)
- Inputs only: `GoldQuote(karat, pricePerGram: Money, asOf)`, `FxQuote(egpPerUsd, asOf)`, `InvestPrices`, `InvestThresholds(minEgp, goldMinCentigrams=25, goldKarat=21)`, `now`.
- `investBand`: S <= 0 nothingSaved; non-EGP S never banded (gold); EGP S <= minEgp small; no matching-karat EGP price goldUnknown; else exact cross-multiplication in BigInt (`S_minor*100*10^expP` vs `cg*price_minor*10^expS`), S >= G gold, else stocks.
- `illustrate` returns `band`, `stocksInGeneral` flag (the app owns the wording) and typed rows: `GoldRow` (21K and 24K when priced, centigrams, date, stale), `UsdRow` (approximate flag; USD source as is), `CertificateRow` (EGP only), `NoPriceRow(instrument, currency)`. small/nothingSaved give no rows; stocks and goldUnknown drop gold (no noPrice row for the dropped gold). Gold only for EGP/USD sources; other currencies get `NoPriceRow`.
- Rounding: BigInt exact, half-even once per figure; exponents honoured per currency. Stale = older than 24 h (exactly 24 h is fresh), still used.
- Instruments are the closed enum gold/usd/certificate; no index, no free text.
