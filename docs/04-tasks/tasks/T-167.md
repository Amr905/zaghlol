## Description
Correct Zakah estimates per asset lot.

## Technical details
Umm al-Qura Hijri table; lots from acquisition; nisab 85 g 24K; 21K 87.5% purity; 2.5% per eligible lot in native unit + EGP cost; short-term debts; ledger statuses.

## Design note
As built (short note; full comments in `packages/zaghlol_domain/lib/zakah/zakah_engine.dart`):
- Lots are native (currency minor units; gold in milligrams; coin in milli-coins). Dues are per lot in its own unit, 25/1000 rounded once. Cost column = due valued at the gold price / converted by the caller's `ZakahMarket.convert`, or null (never guessed).
- The only cross-unit sum is the nisab test (all lots valued in the 24K price's currency); missing price or rate gives `needsPrices` and no dues.
- Hawl counted in days from `hawlStart`; `nextHawlStart` = continuation after a settled cycle; `paid` carries the ledger amount (FR-ZAK-04). Hijri date (tabular) is display/storage only.
- Not built here: ledger persistence/wiring (FU-096 `recordPaidMoney`, T-168), assets-to-lots mapping, ARB copy.

## For the scholar (OQ-10 / BR-ZAK-05)
Every choice below is a `ZakahRules` field or a documented behaviour; SRS default shown.
1. Nisab by 85 g of 24K gold (SRS). Silver nisab (595 g, lower threshold) is not built; the engine would need a silver price. Gold vs silver vs lower-of-the-two is open.
2. Hawl = 354 days from each lot's acquisition (SRS); `hawlDays` is a parameter. Counted in days, not Hijri months; the Hijri date shown is tabular, not Umm al-Qura (may differ by a day).
3. Nisab must hold only on the test day (SRS). Classical rule that the hawl resets if wealth drops below nisab during the year is NOT applied.
4. Lots still in hawl count toward the nisab test; each lot is due only when its own hawl completes (SRS).
5. Debts: only short-term debts due now, deducted from cash of the same currency, oldest lot first; excess debt is not carried to gold, stocks or other currencies (BR-ZAK-04). Whether debts may reduce other assets is open.
6. 21K counts 87.5% (karat/24) toward nisab (SRS); 22K and 18K use karat/24 too; the 8 g coin counts as 8 g of 21K. Not stated in the SRS for 22K/18K/coin.
7. Rate 2.5% on the lot value, rounded half-even to the unit's smallest step (minor unit; milligram for gold) (rounding is not in the SRS). Rounding up is an alternative.
8. Stocks and funds are zakatable at 2.5% of market value (spec §11 trading stocks); long-term investment stocks (dividend or liquid-asset ratio) are not modelled. Certificates count at principal.
9. A cycle left unpaid for several years is not accumulated: the due is 2.5% of the current balance once; arrears are not tracked.
10. Jewelry and personal-use gold: the lot's `zakatable` flag is the user's choice; the engine does not decide.
11. A lot whose value can't be priced makes the whole result `needsPrices` (no dues stated).

### As shipped in T-168 (differs from the engine)
Items 5 and 10 are engine only, not wired in the app.
- Debts: none are read, so none are deducted (item 5 is not wired). The screen says so and tells the user to ask a scholar.
- Every asset is counted as zakatable. There is no opt-out for personal-use jewelry (item 10 is not wired).
- An account balance's hawl starts on the day the account was added to the app.
- The balance used for an account is the last parsed balance.
- An asset's hawl starts at its `acquired_at`, or else the day it was added.
- Certificates count at principal; funds count at the manual value.
- Excluded asset types: real estate, cars and others.
