## Description
`PlanValidator.Validate(planJson, sentText, lang) → Valid(plan, template?) | Fallback(reasonCode)`: (1) JSON Schema (JsonSchema.Net over the contract file); (2) every `ref` occurs verbatim in the sent text and no plain bundled-registry alias appears in any string; (3) `mention` is a substring of the normalised sent text; (4) amount rule; (5) cross-field rules; (6) template grammar (failure drops `t` only). Reason codes are a closed enum.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every vector in `ask-plan-v1.json` gives its expected outcome and reason code.
2. Each §2.3 rejection class has a test: digit (Western and Arabic-Indic), `⟦`, stray `{`, `@`, `://`, `www.`, `label.tld`, markup characters, bidi override, two emoji, ar < 70 % Arabic letters, en with an Arabic letter, a blank not allowed for the plan, a blank used twice, > 200 chars.
3. Template failure keeps the plan (`Valid` with `t = null`); steps 1–5 failing → `Fallback`.
4. Validation never logs or throws with plan text; reason codes only (test with a log sink).
