## Description
Gold per karat per gram (EGP, USD) and FX, served from cache.

## Technical details
- `IPriceProvider`; adapter for the contracted local gold-market feed (D-41; contract pending — build against the interface plus a fake until credentials exist); FX adapter (licensed source); hourly worker; karat derivation where the feed lacks a karat; stale flag.
- Prices are `numeric` in the DB and decimal strings on the wire with a separate ISO 4217 `currency`, never JSON numbers.
- **Operator override (§4.8):** `PUT /admin/v1/prices/override` takes `{symbol, currency, price, reason, expiresAt?}`; `expiresAt` defaults to 24 h and is capped at 7 days (`price_quotes.override_expires_at`); after expiry the feed applies again; audited.
