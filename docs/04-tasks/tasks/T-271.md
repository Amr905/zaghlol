## Scope
1. A salary credit (salary template event, generic salary wording, or user-marked salary) in a non-primary currency is stored as income with the salary category in its own currency (check `salary_matching.dart`, `salary_rules.dart`, categorization and the Arab Bank USD salary path from batch 42 — fix whichever step drops or reclassifies it).
2. Payday detection (`packages/zaghlol_domain/lib/salary/payday_detection.dart`, `payday_auto_service.dart`): replace "primary currency only" with "the user's salary currency": the currency of the latest 3 months' salary credits with the most months; ties → primary currency. Two salaries in different currencies in one month: the first salary of the month still decides the day.
3. Home/month income: the salary shows under its currency (per-currency totals; no silent conversion; the existing "≈" view may include it, labelled approximate). Salary expectations (`salary.state`) accept the currency.
4. No schema change expected; if one is needed, stop and write why in the task file.

## Acceptance criteria
Domain tests: USD salaries 24/23/24 → payday 23 with EGP as primary; mixed EGP+USD months; app test: Arab Bank USD salary SMS → income, salary category, USD, payday auto-set banner; `flutter analyze` clean.
