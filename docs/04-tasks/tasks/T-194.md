## Description
When the user switches the app to dark mode, Captain Zaghlol shows a playful line: **«يعني تبقى فلوسك والمود؟»** (PO's wording, written as the PO typed it: «يعني تبقي فلوسك والمود؟»; T-058's Egyptian reader may polish spelling, not the joke). English: a light equivalent, e.g. "Dark mode? Your money and your mood, both in the dark?" (ux copy to confirm).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Shown once each time the user picks Dark (or System when the result is dark) in settings, as the mascot snackbar/toast used elsewhere; not shown when the OS flips the theme on its own, not on app start.
2. Strings in both ARB files, no hard-coded text; works in RTL and LTR; respects Reduce Motion.
3. Widget test: picking Dark shows the line once; picking Light shows nothing.
