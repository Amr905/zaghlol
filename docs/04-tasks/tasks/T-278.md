## Today
`app/lib/features/home/presentation/home_screen.dart` shows one full `PageView` page per currency (chips + dots); `home_read_model.dart` builds `HomeModel.currencies` (`HomeCurrencyFigures`, ordered by `orderCurrencies`).

## Scope
1. The **primary currency page (EGP) is always the only hero page**. Remove the per-currency pager, chips and dots.
2. Under the hero, a row titled «فلوسك بعملات تانية» of **small currency cards**, one per non-primary currency that **had any activity this month (spending, income or an exchange) or has a non-zero balance on any account**. Each card: ISO code, what is left (`CycleTotals.left`, exact, in that currency's exponent), and this month's money in / out on a second line. Ordered by most recent activity; at most 3, then a `+N` chip that opens a sheet listing the rest.
3. Tap a card → that currency's view (route `/currency/:iso`, built in T-279). Until T-279 is merged, navigation goes through one helper `openCurrencyView(context, currency)` in `app/lib/features/home/presentation/currency_cards.dart`.
4. A user with only EGP sees no currency UI at all (no title, no empty row).
5. Leave an empty spot right under the cards for the ≈ line (T-280 builds `HomeApproxLine`; do **not** build it here). Expose it as `homeApproxSlotBuilder` (a Riverpod `Provider<WidgetBuilder?>` defaulting to null) so the Lead can plug T-280 in at merge without touching layout.
6. Read model: add what the cards need (per-currency `left`, in/out, last activity, balance-only currencies) to `HomeModel` without changing the EGP figures.
7. Strings ar (Egyptian) + en from plan §6 (`home.currencyCards.title`, `home.currencyCard.left`, `home.currencyCard.more`), screen-reader labels, RTL, 1.3× text, light/dark.

## Acceptance criteria
- PO story (plan §2): USD salary 1,000 and exchange 600 → EGP 29,700 → Home shows the EGP hero (money in 29,700 «منهم … من تغيير عملة») and one USD card «فاضل USD ٤٠٠٫٠٠». Widget test.
- Card rule: activity-only currency, balance-only currency, and a currency with neither (no card). Ordering + `+N` overflow with 5 currencies.
- EGP-only user: no currency UI (widget test).
- Goldens ar/en light/dark for Home with 2 cards (regenerate through the app-regen workflow, never local PNGs).
- Existing Home tests updated, not deleted, where the pager is gone.
