## Description
Own-account movements never count as spending.

## Technical details
Pair opposite same-currency equal amounts within 5 min across user accounts; ambiguity → review (EC-16); late-arrival re-check; one-sided internal (EC-15); fx_conversion and cc_payment pairs; cross-currency suggestion (±3%, 3 days); manual link/unlink.

## Design note
Short note by the implementer (none existed; PO asked for no per-task design round on C3).

- **Split.** Pure rules in `packages/zaghlol_domain/lib/transfers/transfer_matching.dart` (`decideSameCurrency`, `fxConversionCandidates`, `crossCurrencyCandidates`, `convertWithRate`, `withinTolerance`). Persistence in `app/lib/features/transactions/application/transfer_pairing.dart` (`TransferPairingUseCase`: `onPersisted`, `link`, `unlink`). No schema change; uses `transfer_pairs`, `transactions.is_internal/transfer_pair_id`, `review_items`.
- **Never pair on doubt.** One candidate pairs only if the new leg is also that candidate's only candidate; otherwise `transfer_ambiguous` (EC-16). Cross-currency is a suggestion only (`fx_pair_suggestion`, 3 days, 3% of the converted amount, integer BigInt math, direct rate or the inverse of the opposite rate; no rate means no suggestion). Only `posted`, live `transfer_out`/`transfer_in` legs take part.
- **Kinds.** `auto`; `card_settlement` when the in leg's account is credit/BNPL (or an own-account out leg names a credit/BNPL account's last4); `fx_conversion` for opposite legs of different currencies in one `multi_currency` account within 5 min (if a rate is known and contradicts the amounts, a suggestion instead); `manual`/`cross_currency` from `link`.
- **One-sided (EC-15).** An own-account out leg with no match gets a pair row with `in_txn_id` NULL and is internal; a later matching in leg completes the same pair. An own-account in leg is only flagged internal (the schema's pair row needs an out leg).
- **Concurrency.** Each call is one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction; every write is conditional on the leg being live and unpaired; UNIQUE `out_txn_id`/`in_txn_id` give one winner; the loser rolls back (`skipped`, or `XFR_ALREADY_PAIRED` for `link`).
- **Events.** After commit: `TransactionsChanged` with both legs' accounts and cycles plus the cycles of any expense linked by an offset link (FU-110); `MascotTrigger(detective, self_transfer)` for kind `auto` only. `loadSchedule` is required (FU-114 77-1a).
- **FU-114 77-3.** Bulk `SetAccount` refuses rows that have a `transfer_pair_id` (`BULK_PAIRED`; nothing changes); unlink first.
- **Residuals.** Unlink does not block a later re-pair if one of the legs is edited and `onPersisted` runs again. Soft-deleting one leg of a pair leaves the other leg internal (to review with T-090/T-077 follow-ups). A repair sweep over legs that were never evaluated is the wiring task's job.
