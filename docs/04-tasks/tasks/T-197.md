## Description
`AccountKind.credit` and `AccountKind.debit` exist, but the Money tab and card lists look the same for both, so the user can't see the difference quickly.

## Technical details
- Each card row/tile shows a type chip: **ائتمان / Credit** or **خصم مباشر / Debit** (also Prepaid, Wallet, BNPL where relevant), plus a distinct icon.
- Credit cards show **used / limit** with a small bar and the statement due date; debit cards show the **balance**. Group the Money tab by type (Bank accounts & debit cards · Credit cards · Wallets & prepaid · Cash) or offer that as a sort.
- The same chip appears in the card filter, transaction detail ("CIB •1334 · ائتمان") and card detail header.
- Colour is never the only cue (chip text + icon).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Every card surface shows its type; credit shows used/limit and due date when known.
2. Grouping/sort works and persists.
3. ar/en, light/dark goldens of the Money tab with mixed card types; RTL verified; no hard-coded strings.

## Scope notes (review 008b4720)
- The type label is **not** added to the compact list rows (`txn_row`), the ingestion review, the merge flow or the capture settings: there the card label + last 4 stays. The transaction detail shows the type ("CIB •1334 · Credit"); the Money tab, card detail header, card filters and pickers show the chip or the type text.
- Credit rows show "used of limit" and the bar only when the limit and the available figure are in the same currency. Over the limit shows used above limit with a full bar; at 90 % or more the bar uses the warning tone (the text carries the meaning too). While amounts are hidden the used share is not spoken.
