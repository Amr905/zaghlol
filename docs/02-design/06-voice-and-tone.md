# 06 — Voice & Tone: Egyptian Arabic first (المصري أولاً)

Decision **D-34** (2026-11-01): the whole Arabic app speaks **Egyptian colloquial Arabic (العامية المصرية)** — not only Captain Zaghlol. The goal: fun, warm and easy for most Egyptians, regardless of age or education.

## 1. Principles
| # | Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|-----------|----|-------|
| 1 | **Talk like a friend from Cairo** | "مسموحلك تصرف النهارده" · "فاضلك" · "شوف الكل" | MSA like "المبلغ المتاح للإنفاق اليومي" |
| 2 | **Clear beats clever** | Jokes in mascot lines and empty states | Jokes in buttons, errors, money confirmations, permissions |
| 3 | **Short** | Buttons ≤ 3 words: "يلا نبدأ" · "احفظ" · "تمام" | Long buttons or sentences on chips |
| 4 | **Warm, never shaming** | "حاسب يا كبير! الأكل بره قرب يخلص" | Insults about the person, family, body, religion, region |
| 5 | **Widely understood Egyptian** | Common Cairene words everyone knows | Heavy street slang, regional jokes, English slang mixes beyond common ones (أوردر، كارت، دليفري) |
| 6 | **Respect money moments** | Calm, precise copy on payments, Gold purchase, backup passphrase, delete | Emojis or jokes on destructive/financial confirmations |
| 7 | **Gender-friendly** | Default masculine-generic as Egyptians commonly speak ("إنت", "يا كبير"); avoid gendered forms where an easy neutral exists ("اختار براحتك") | — |

Where standard Arabic stays: **legal texts** (terms, privacy policy), **store listings' legal lines**, **financial disclaimers** (Zakah estimate, "not financial advice") — written in simple standard Arabic with an Egyptian one-line summary above them.

## 2. Tone by context
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Captain Zaghlol (banners, chat, chart explanations) | Playful, witty, encouraging | «عاش يا وحش! وفرت ٢,٢٠٠ زيادة عن الشهر اللي فات 👏» |
| Roast mode | Cheeky, never cruel; about spending only | «إمسك أعصابك يا فنان! الفلوس دي كانت تجيبلك سبيكة دهب» |
| Navigation & labels | Friendly, plain | الرئيسية · الحسبة · فلوسي · زغلول |
| Buttons | Action words | يلا نبدأ · احفظ · تمام · سيبها · اتدفع · يلا نشترك |
| Errors | Calm, reassuring, what to do next | «مش قادر أوصل للسيرفر دلوقتي — فلوسك في أمان على موبايلك، جرب تاني كمان شوية» |
| Permissions | Honest, specific | «بقرا رسايل البنوك بس — رسايلك الشخصية عمري ما ببص عليها» |
| Destructive actions | Serious, explicit | «هتمسح كل حاجة من على الموبايل ده. مفيش رجوع.» |
| Success | Short + light emoji | «اتسجلوا ٣ حركات ✓» |

## 4. Numbers, money & emoji
- Arabic-Indic digits by default (٣١٤ ج.م); user can switch to 123 (A-29).
- Currency word: «ج.م» in figures, «جنيه» in sentences.
- Emoji: max one per message, only in mascot lines, greetings, success and empty states — never in errors, prices or legal text.

## 5. English
English remains a full option, in a friendly casual tone ("Safe to spend today", "You're on track!"), written fresh — not a literal translation of the Egyptian jokes.

## 6. Review rule
Every Arabic string in the copy deck is reviewed by a native Egyptian speaker for naturalness and for anything that could feel mocking or exclusionary before release.
