## 3. Amount colour rule (replaces 04 §1.4)
Spending is shown in **ink** with a minus sign (`−450 ج.م`), not red — calmer for an app people open every day. Money in uses `incomeText` with a plus. Red/terracotta is reserved for problems (over budget, errors) so it keeps its meaning. Internal transfers stay muted with the ⇄ icon.

## 4. Component refinements ("easy on the eyes")
| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Surfaces | No outlines; separation by surface tone + very soft shadow (`0 6 20 / 5%`). Radius 24 for cards/lists, 28 for hero |
| Hero | Light **mesh gradient** (teal mist + jade + gold glow) with ink text and the key number in primary, instead of a heavy dark teal block; KPIs sit on a translucent strip |
| Progress | 10 dp, fully rounded, warm track; gold gradient for cycle progress, terracotta only when ≥ 85% |
| Chips | Borderless pills (34 dp) on surface; selected = teal mist |
| Buttons | 54 dp pills; secondary = teal mist with teal text (no grey) |
| Zaghlol's voice | Banners and chat answers use the lavender surface — users learn "lavender = Zaghlol talking" |
| Category tiles | Circles, unified pastel tints |
| Tab bar | Warm glass (surface at 78% + blur), active tab teal mist; gold gradient Add button |
| Typography | Large title 30/700; hero number 46/700; body 16 — unchanged scale, softer weights on secondary text |

## 5. Logo & app icon (updated: D-34)
**Concept — the funny Captain Zaghlol holding coins:** the smiling, thumbs-up pigeon from the spec artwork, arms full of gold coins, on a deep Nile-teal icon with a warm cream halo behind him. Fun, Egyptian, instantly about money.

Rules: keep the cream halo behind the bird on teal; don't recolour him; minimum icon size 29 px. In-app, this pose is also the welcome image, chat avatar and voice-logger face; the other poses (shocked, detective, professor) stay as his expressions.

**Production note:** the artwork is upscaled from the spec's image (≈ 300 px source). Before store submission, commission a high-resolution or vector redraw (also needed for the Rive animations); the icon layout stays the same.
